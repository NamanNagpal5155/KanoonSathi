Bar Registry Guideline: Avoiding 8(1) Rejections

Under Section 6(1), always ask for concrete *recorded material* (e.g. "certified copies of letters, vouchers, logs") rather than interrogative questions like "Why was the road not built?". PIOs are only mandated to provide existing records, not opinions.

Citizen Rights Protocol: Timeline & Escalation Matrix

₹10

Fee & IPO Details

Purchase a ₹10 Indian Postal Order (IPO) or Court Fee stamp from any Indian Post Office. If you possess a valid BPL (Below Poverty Line) card, you are 100% exempt from all RTI fees under Section 7(5).

30d

30-Day Response Mandate

The Public Information Officer (PIO) is statutorily bound to provide information within 30 days of receipt. If the matter concerns life or liberty, the deadline shrinks to 48 hours under Section 7(1).

§19

First Appeal Protocol

If no reply is received within 30 days, or if unsatisfactory information is supplied, file a First Appeal under Section 19(1) to the designated First Appellate Authority (FAA) within 30 days without fee.
