Constitutional Architecture of Indian Judicature

How to Determine Which Court Has Jurisdiction Over Your Matter?

Under Indian Jurisprudence, filing before a court lacking territorial or pecuniary competence leads to rejection of plaint under Order VII Rule 11 of the CPC. Follow this verified sovereign ladder:

1

Magistrate / Munsif

Civil suits of lower valuation (up to ₹1-3 Lakhs) and criminal complaints punishable by up to 3 years imprisonment.

2

District & Sessions

Principal court of original civil and criminal jurisdiction in the district. Hears appeals from magistrate decisions and serious offenses.

3

High Court (State)

Constitutional writ petitions under Article 226, first appeals, criminal appeals, and supervisory jurisdiction over all subordinate courts.

4

Supreme Court

Apex Court of India under Article 32 (Fundamental Rights) and Special Leave Petitions (SLP) under Article 136. Tilak Marg, New Delhi.
