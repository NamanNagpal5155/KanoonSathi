## Brand & Style

This design system translates the dignity, gravitas, and institutional authority of Indian jurisprudence into an accessible, human-first digital instrument. It rejects cold, sterile corporate legal interfaces in favor of a tactile, editorial presence rooted in sovereign legal manuscripts, parliamentary gazettes, and constitutional prints, executed with modern structural precision.

### Personality & Values
- **Authoritative & Constitutional:** Rooted in the timeless legitimacy of Indian legal history, evoking the weight of supreme judicial decrees through symmetrical layout architecture and classical typographic hierarchy.
- **Dignified & Human-Centric:** Built for citizens seeking justice, advocates navigating case dockets, and paralegals executing filings. The interface is composed, clear, and reassuring without condescension or impenetrable legalese.
- **Tactile Heritage:** Echoes the physical textures of heavy rag paper, archival registry ledgers, blind-embossed seals, and warm brass fittings found in venerable bar association libraries.

### Design Movement: Classical Editorial Sovereign Tech
The visual execution fuses **Editorial Sovereign Heritage** with **Precise Structural Modernism**. High-contrast, stately serif headings establish institutional trust, grounded by structured modern grotesk body typography. Surfaces rely on warm cotton rag archival tones, subtle double-ruled hairpins, watermarked geometric framing, and restrained metallic accents rather than loud, synthetic gradients.

## Colors

The palette directly references the tactile components of Indian judicial culture: archival stamp ink, handmade stamp papers, sacred saffron threads, burnished brass emblems, and institutional indigo robes.

### Functional Roles

- **Primary (`#1A2744` Judicial Indigo):** The voice of supreme authority. Applied to key navigation frames, primary action triggers, institutional seals, and authoritative headlines. Deep shade `#0F192C` anchors heavy headers and contrast-critical touchpoints.
- **Secondary (`#C35619` Sovereign Saffron / Terracotta):** Represents the living constitutional spark, urgent notice, active judicial dockets, and high-priority interactive highlights. Softened tone `#D96B27` provides hover and focus illumination.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A869` Antique Brass / Metallic Gold):** Conveys prestige, sovereign certification, and delicate archival rules. Used for double-line border flourishes, verified counsel badges, watermarks, and authenticated document stamps.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF9F4` Archival Ivory & `#F4EFE6` Warm Rag Paper):** Replaces clinical cool-white displays with the warmth of heavy cotton rag document sheets. Creates high contrast against `#111317` (Deep Charcoal Ink), ensuring effortless legibility during marathon document readings.
- **Semantic Green (`#3E6548` Olive Sage):** Signals resolved petitions, favorable rulings, and secure cryptographic verifications without the jarring neon of modern software success states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances sovereign solemnity with high-density data utility:

- **Headlines & Declarations:** `Playfair Display` commands attention with high stroke contrast, elegant serifs, and historic gravitas reminiscent of original gazettes, formal affidavits, and constitutional preambles.
- **Body & Data Grid:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides pristine clarity, high legibility at dense scales, and a contemporary cadence for case briefs, statutory clauses, and filing metadata.
- **Bilingual & Script Support:** When presenting Hindi, Sanskrit terms, or vernacular statutory copies, pair directly with `Noto Sans Devanagari` set at matching scale ratios, ensuring unified baseline alignment across mixed-script legal text.
- **Typographic Rules:** All uppercase legal badges and section labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) utilize expanded letter-spacing to mirror formal archival stamps and embossed docket seals.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow strict structural symmetry balanced with generous breathing room, inspired by the proportioned margins of judicial parchment and courtroom transcripts.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `3rem` canvas margins, and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers lock at a max-width of `1280px` for balanced reading rhythm.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Case files and petition logs collapse into dual-column cards.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Structural manuscript lines and dual borders streamline into single hairline framing to safeguard vertical rhythm and viewport space.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical distances prioritize deliberate pacing. Document headers, affidavit cards, and judicial decision blocks use `space-xl` margins to encourage calm, considered scrutiny.
- Form inputs, procedural metadata, and court timeline events use crisp, consistent `space-sm` and `space-md` element spacing to maintain dense information readability.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eschews synthetic, floaty drop shadows in favor of paper-layered depth, subtle tonal stepping, and tactile physical presence.

### Tactile Layering Principles
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** Set to `#FBF9F4` (Cotton Parchment). Gives documents and lists an immediate warm, archival physical presence.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card & Docket Containers):** White `#FFFFFF` or pale ivory `#FDFCF9` layered directly over the canvas, defined primarily by a fine `1px solid rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.35)` antique brass rule or a muted border `rgba(17, 19, 23, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Active Modals, Flying Menus & Case Binders):** Rendered with a warm, diffuse ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 39, 68, 0.08), 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(17, 19, 23, 0.06);`
  This tint reflects the deep indigo ink and charcoal grounding, not cold grey.
- **The Blind Emboss & Stamp Depth:** Selected verified cards and legal certifications feature a delicate inner shadow (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(17, 19, 23, 0.06)`) combined with double-line gold framing to simulate raised seal impressions.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** structural character. 

- Sharp, authoritative corners are softened with micro-radii (`0.25rem` / `4px`) for buttons, inputs, and tabs, reflecting precision-cut parchment paper rather than rounded plastic.
- Large dossier containers, judgment panels, and document viewers utilize `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`), retaining an orderly, architectural outline.
- Circular geometry is reserved strictly for judicial seals, user profile sigils, and sovereign iconography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign Button:** Rich Judicial Indigo background (`#1A2744`), crisp ivory text (`#FBF9F4`), `4px` corner radius, `12px 24px` padding. Styled with a delicate `1px` inner border tint (`rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.3)`). Hover activates a subtle lift with deep indigo glow: `rgba(26, 39, 68, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary Urgent Action:** Sovereign Terracotta background (`#C35619`), white text, bold label typography. Used for filing petitions, summoning counsel, or paying court stamp duties.
- **Tertiary Archival Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid #C5A869`, charcoal text (`#111317`). Hover shifts background to translucent antique brass tint (`rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.1)`).

### Cards & Dockets
- **Judgment & Case Card:** Ivory surface backed by an elegant left border accent: a `4px` solid bar of Judicial Indigo or Sovereign Saffron denoting case urgency.
- **Framed Legal Affidavit Card:** Features an inner perimeter double-hairline border (`1px solid rgba(197, 168, 105, 0.4)` set `4px` inward from the outer card edge), delivering immediate court-document recognition.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Legal Form Field:** Archival Ivory background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid rgba(17, 19, 23, 0.16)`, `4px` border radius, padding `12px 16px`. Active focus shifts border to Judicial Indigo (`#1A2744`) with an ambient outer ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(26, 39, 68, 0.12)`.
- **Statute Reference Search:** Integrated with watermark-style prefix icons (scales of justice, gavel, or section marker `§`) in antique brass.

### Status Chips & Seals
- **Verified Advocate Seal:** Compact badge utilizing an antique brass double border, `#1A2744` text, and a circular metallic verification mark.
- **Hearing Stage Chips:** Subdued pill markers with low-saturation backgrounds:
  - *Pending Notice:* Warm Ochre background (`#FFF8EC`), Terracotta text (`#C35619`).
  - *Disposed / Dismissed:* Muted Ash background (`#ECEEF1`), Indigo text (`#1A2744`).
  - *Decreed / Verified:* Olive Sage background (`#EFF5F0`), Deep Green text (`#2A4731`).

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted tactile feel. Unselected: `1.5px solid rgba(17, 19, 23, 0.3)`. Selected: filled with Judicial Indigo (`#1A2744`) with sharp geometric tick/center marks in ivory.

### Specialized Component: The Digital Vakalatnama Ribbon
- A distinctive component for document authenticity: a ribbon header spanning the top edge of official legal summaries, displaying gold thread patterning, court registration IDs, and bilingual Ashoka-inspired balance markers.
