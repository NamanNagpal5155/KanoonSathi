Who Can Get Free Legal Aid? (विधिक सहायता की पात्रता)

Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 specifies citizens entitled to free legal counsel, court fee exemption, and docket representation.

Income Exemption Cap: ₹3,00,000 / year (Delhi) Categorical exemptions bypass income limits.
family_restroom
NO INCOME CEILING

Women & Children

All women and minors are entitled to completely free legal services in civil, matrimonial, POCSO, and criminal proceedings regardless of financial earnings.

UNIVERSAL AID

SC / ST Communities

Members of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes receive state-funded legal aid and trial counsel representation automatically under Section 12(a).

Industrial Laborers & Workmen

Factory laborers, daily-wage construction workers, gig economy delivery partners, and domestic workers disputing wages or unfair dismissal.

Persons with Disabilities

Litigants with physical, sensory, or mental disabilities as defined in the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
