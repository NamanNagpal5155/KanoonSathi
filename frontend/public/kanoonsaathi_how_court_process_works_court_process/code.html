STAGE 05 OF 07 Order XVIII Civil Procedure Code, 1908 & BSA, 2023

Stage 05: Evidence (साक्ष्य एवं गवाही)

timer Benchmark: 4 - 9 Months

gavel What Happens at This Stage

In a civil suit, the Evidence stage is where each party establishes their assertions through sworn testimony and admitted documentary proof. The Plaintiff commences with their evidence (P.W. 1, P.W. 2), followed by the Defendant's evidence (D.W. 1, D.W. 2).

1. Examination-in-Chief
Witness submits their signed evidence affidavit (Order XVIII Rule 4 CPC). No oral examination is typically needed.
2. Cross-Examination
Opposing counsel interrogates the witness orally before the Judge or Local Commissioner to test veracity and expose contradictions.
3. Re-Examination
Your advocate may briefly re-examine to clarify ambiguous matters raised during cross-examination (with court permission).

Electronic Records Rule: Under Section 63 & 65B of the Indian Evidence Act (now Section 61 & 63 of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023), printouts of emails, WhatsApp chats, server logs, or banking SMSs are inadmissible unless accompanied by a mandatory statutory certificate executed by the custodian of the computer device.

Sworn statutory certificate stating device details, SHA-256 cryptographic hash if digital, and confirmation of unhampered lawful custody.

forum Essential Questions to Ask Your Advocate (वकील से पूछने योग्य प्रश्न)

help

"Have all electronic records (WhatsApp chats, emails, recordings) been paired with a certified Section 65B affidavit?"

Why this matters: Without this affidavit submitted at the time of leading evidence, the opposing counsel can have critical digital exhibits de-marked or declared inadmissible.

help

"Which independent or official witnesses (bank manager, sub-registrar) must be summoned via court process?"

Why this matters: Official witnesses must be issued court summons (diet money / batta deposited) at least 3 weeks before the hearing date.

help

"Is our cross-examination being recorded before the Presiding Judge directly or before a Court Commissioner?"

Why this matters: Order XVIII Rule 4 allows evidence recording through a retired judge or advocate commissioner, which can drastically expedite the process.
