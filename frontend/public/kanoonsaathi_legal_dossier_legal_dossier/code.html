Core Dispute: The complainant, Rajesh V. Sharma, leased residential premises at Flat 402, Mayur Vihar Phase-I, New Delhi from the respondent landlord, Sh. Ramesh Goel, under an 11-month registered lease with a refundable security deposit of ₹45,000.

Upon standard vacation of the premises on 31 January 2025, keys were surrendered with zero damage acknowledged via signed written inspection. The respondent arbitrarily failed to remit the refundable security amount within the statutory 30-day window, citing unspecified prospective maintenance.

A formal statutory Legal Notice under §106 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882 was dispatched via Speed Post RPAD on 14 February 2025 and served on 18 February 2025. The mandatory 15-day restitution cure period lapsed without remittance. The matter is currently formatted for the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (DCDRC-II, Delhi) on grounds of deficiency of service and unfair trade practice.

04. Legal Issues for Adjudication (विधिक मुद्दे)

Framed for DCDRC-II Bench
gavel ISSUE OF LAW 01 — CONSUMER JURISDICTION & SERVICE DEFICIENCY

Whether the arbitrary and mala fide withholding of a refundable tenancy security deposit post lawful vacation constitutes a "Deficiency of Service" and "Unfair Trade Practice" under Section 2(11) & Section 2(47) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019?

Litigant Contention: Landlord acts in the capacity of a housing housing service provider whose contractual obligation includes prompt return of trust funds upon delivery of vacant possession. Unsubstantiated withholding without bill of deductions constitutes actionable deficiency.

percent ISSUE OF LAW 02 — STATUTORY RESTITUTION & PENAL INTEREST

Whether the Complainant is legally entitled to statutory compensatory interest at the rate of 18% per annum from the date of handover (31 Jan 2025) until actual realization, alongside litigation costs?

Litigant Contention: Relying on Supreme Court precedents in consumer jurisprudence, money wrongfully retained by another party unjustly enriches the holder; commercial interest of 18% p.a. is fair and warranted.
